Upload LanDrop-google-play-512.png as the App icon in your Google Play main store listing.
It uses the existing Android logo on a full white square. Google Play applies the rounded mask and outer shadow.

Original logo copies:
- LanDrop-logo-dark.svg: dark symbol, transparent background
- LanDrop-logo-white.svg: white symbol, transparent background
- LanDrop-android-launcher.svg / -192.png: existing rounded Android launcher artwork
- LanDrop-windows-logo-512.png / -icon.ico: existing Windows assets
- LanDrop-google-play.svg: vector source of the Play listing export

Google Play requirements: 512 x 512, 32-bit PNG, sRGB, at most 1024 KB.
https://developer.android.com/distribute/google-play/resources/icon-design-specifications
